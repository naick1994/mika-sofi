Mika

One of the most successful kitesurfers of all time. A 9-time World Champion across Freestyle and Big Air, riding at the top level since she was thirteen. Born on the coast of Ceará, Brazil, Mika combines raw talent with relentless daily training, and is known for pushing the limits of women's kitesurfing.
